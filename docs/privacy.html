ContentForge Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

ContentForge is a desktop publishing tool used to connect authorized social-media accounts and publish content selected by the user.

Information processed

ContentForge processes OAuth authorization tokens, account identifiers, and the media and captions the user chooses to publish. OAuth tokens are stored locally in the user's macOS Keychain.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the user with a selected platform, verify the connected account, and carry out publishing actions the user initiates or approves.

Sharing

ContentForge sends data only to the social-media platforms the user connects and to service providers required for the selected publishing action. It does not sell personal information.

Control

You may disconnect an account through ContentForge or the relevant platform and may remove local app data from your device.
